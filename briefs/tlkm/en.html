Telkom Indonesia (IDX: TLKM, NYSE: TLK)

Investor brief · price Rp1,960 (2026-11-02) · figures from IDX filings unless stated
Thesis. Indonesia's dominant telecom operator is now a cash machine: capex has fallen from 23% to 13% of revenue, free cash flow pays a ~11% dividend, and the reported profit drop is mostly accounting (GoTo / start-up revaluation, depreciation policy), not the business. The share trades near its 2026 low because of Indonesia's MSCI risk, not because of Telkom.

Q3 2026 includes a Rp1.33 trn non-cash revaluation loss (GoTo −Rp0.43 trn, MDI start-ups −Rp0.90 trn); excluding it Q3 ≈ Rp5.0 trn, ≈ +10% y/y. 2025 on the restated basis (drop-cable depreciation); 2023–2024 as originally reported.

Capex is falling; cash covers the dividend (Rp trn)

Capex/revenue: 22.6% (2023) → 17.6% → 16.3% → 12.9% (9M 2026). After leases (Rp7.4 trn) and dividends to minorities (Rp7.4 trn, mainly Singtel in Telkomsel), FY2025 free cash flow to shareholders ≈ Rp26.0 trn vs Rp22.0 trn dividend (1.18x).

How the shareholder gets paid

  1. Dividends: Rp223.17/share for FY2025 (Rp22.0 trn, paid 10 Jul 2026) — 123% of net profit but ≈85% of FY2025 free cash flow to shareholders. The state fund Danantara (52%) relies on this cash.
  2. Buybacks: new Rp4 trn programme approved 8 Jun 2026 (≈2% of market cap) runs to Jun 2027.
  3. Asset monetisation: the fibre business was spun into InfraNexia (Rp49.9 trn transferred on 30 Sep 2026); a stake sale or IPO would crystallise value — terms not yet disclosed.
  4. Re-rating: EV/EBITDA from ~4.8x to 5.5–6x if the MSCI risk fades ⇒ Rp2,600–2,870 per share (own lens), +33–46% from today.

Why upside is possible

  • Cash, not accounting: operating cash flow rose to Rp63.8 trn in 2025 while reported profit fell 20%; the gap is depreciation policy and investment revaluations.
  • Capex cycle down: 4G/fibre build-out largely done; spectrum (700 MHz + 2.6 GHz, 2026 auction) is the next cost, shared across three operators.
  • Telkomsel still leads: B2C segment revenue 9M 2026 +3.5%, segment profit +8.7%; ARPU +9% in H1 2026 while subscribers are cleaned up.
  • Low gearing, long USD: (net debt + leases)/EBITDA ≈0.7x; net USD assets — a weak rupiah does not hurt the balance sheet.

What must hold (risks)

  • Dividend cover is moderate: 1.18x FY2025 free cash flow to shareholders; if capex returns above ~20% of revenue the payout is at risk.
  • Competition: Indosat grows revenue +13% and XLSmart consolidates; Telkom B2C growth below 3% would signal share loss.
  • Investments: GoTo at Rp24 (vs 32 on 30 Sep) means another revaluation loss in Q4; MDI may be sold.
  • State control: risk that Danantara directs InfraNexia proceeds to national projects instead of shareholders.
  • MSCI: decision on Indonesia's status by 30 Jun 2027.
