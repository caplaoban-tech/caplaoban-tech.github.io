Thesis. A debt-free palm-oil planter with Rp8.5 trn of cash — 87% of its market value. The market pays almost nothing for the plantations themselves (EV/EBITDA ≈0.4x) even though profit is up 40% this year, production is rising and the company has announced a Rp750 bn buyback on top of a ~6% dividend.

LSIP share price since 2023

The share trades below the level implied by its cash plus a modest value for the estates.

Profit attributable to shareholders, by quarter (Rp trn)

9M 2026: profit Rp1.74 trn (+40%), revenue +9%, CPO output +12% (227 kt), operating profit +44%. No one-offs; interest on cash ≈11% of pre-tax profit.

Cash keeps piling up (Rp trn)

Capex is a fraction of cash flow; cash grew from Rp4.5 trn to Rp8.5 trn in under three years.

How the shareholder gets paid

  1. Dividends: Rp83/share for FY2025 (30% of profit). On 2026E profit (~Rp2.35 trn) the same payout is ≈Rp100/share.
  2. Buyback: up to Rp750 bn until 31 Dec 2026 — ≈7.6% of market cap, funded from cash.
  3. Cash itself: Rp8.5 trn with no bank debt; a special dividend or larger buyback would release it to all shareholders.
  4. Re-rating: at ~0.4x EV/EBITDA, valuing the estates even at peers' multiples (parent SIMP 1.9x) adds a large premium.

Why upside is possible

  • Growing output: nucleus FFB +7% and better extraction lifted CPO production +12% in 9M 2026.
  • Prices: CPO stays in a higher range on Indonesia's biodiesel programme; a weak rupiah (Rp18,100/$) adds to dollar-linked revenue.
  • Balance sheet: cash covers ~87% of market value — downside is cushioned.
  • Catalyst: the buyback (announced 23 Oct, to 31 Dec 2026) — start of purchases to be confirmed in the October register; the FY2026 dividend comes in mid-2027.

What must hold (risks)

  • Controller: SIMP owns 59.5% and needs cash (SIMP debt ex-LSIP ≈Rp8.5 trn; forest-area fine Rp2.49 trn in escrow). Cash reaches minorities only through dividends and buybacks — any loan or deposit from LSIP to the group would break the thesis.
  • Related-party sales: 76% of revenue goes to SIMP (59% a year ago); the transfer price is the channel to watch.
  • Weather and policy: El Niño cut neighbours' Q3 harvest; export duty and levy changes move realised prices.
  • Market: MSCI reclassification risk for Indonesia (decision by 30 Jun 2027).
